This is a B.Tech CSE 4th Semester project exploring innovations in modern computer hardware — covering processors, memory, storage, GPUs, AI accelerators, quantum computing, and future trends as of 2025. It is designed as a comprehensive educational reference on the current state of computing hardware.
All content is researched from official company announcements (NVIDIA, Intel, AMD, IBM, Apple, TSMC), IEEE technical papers, industry conferences (Hot Chips, ISSCC, IEDM), and reputable technology publications. All hardware specifications reflect the state of the industry as of mid-2025.
